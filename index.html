Studies Sync

Studies Sync is a small personal, single-user tool. It reads the owner's own Google Classroom courses (announcements, coursework and course materials) and downloads the attached files to the owner's own computer, so they can be used for studying.

  • Read-only access. It never posts, submits, edits or deletes anything.
  • It is used only by its author, and it is not offered to other users.
  • Data stays on the owner's computer.
